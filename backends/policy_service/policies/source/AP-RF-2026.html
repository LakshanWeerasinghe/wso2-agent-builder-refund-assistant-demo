Asia-Pacific Regional Refund Addendum

Regional rules for our hotels, resorts and ryokan in Japan, Singapore, Thailand and India.

1. Purpose and scope

AP-1.1 This addendum applies to all ReverieEscapes properties in Japan (JP), Singapore (SG), Thailand (TH) and India (IN): Kyoto Gion, Tokyo Ginza, Singapore Marina, Bangkok Riverside, Phuket Kata Beach and Mumbai Marine Drive.

AP-1.2 It supplements the Global Guest Refund and Compensation Policy (GLB-RF-2026). Country addenda and hotel rules prevail over it for the matters they address (GLB-2.1); where it is silent, the global policy applies. Hotel rules for Japanese properties are issued in Japanese, which is their authoritative language (GLB-2.5).

2. Service charges and indirect taxes

AP-2.1 At properties that levy a service charge (Singapore, Thailand and India), the service charge is refunded proportionally on the refunded room revenue: the refund includes the same percentage of service charge as was charged on the room rate.

AP-2.2 Goods and services tax and value added tax (Singapore GST, Thai VAT, Indian GST) on refunded amounts are adjusted on the tax invoice by Finance and are not part of the compensation amount.

AP-2.3 Room rates in Japan include consumption tax. Municipal accommodation taxes in Japan are refunded only where hotel rules provide for it.

3. Weather warnings

AP-3.1 Where a guest departs early because of a government weather warning for the property's area (typhoon, monsoon or tropical cyclone warnings, or warnings issued by the Japan Meteorological Agency), the unused nights are refunded in full.

AP-3.2 The guest informs the front desk before departure, and the property records the warning in force (issuing authority, level and time) in the incident log. Nights the guest actually stays are compensated only where another rule provides.

AP-3.3 Apart from AP-3.1, weather and its consequences remain external causes under GLB-5.7 unless a country, hotel or seasonal rule provides otherwise.

4. Language of decisions

AP-4.1 Refund decisions for Japanese properties are issued in Japanese and English. Where the guest's preferred language is neither, the decision is also provided in that language.

AP-4.2 For properties in Singapore, Thailand and India, decisions are issued in English and, on request, in the guest's preferred language (GLB-9.1).

5. Approval authority

AP-5.1 Approval thresholds follow GLB-7, with booking-currency equivalents calculated at the policy reference rates in Appendix A of GLB-RF-2026. Hotel and seasonal documents may set local thresholds, which then apply (GLB-7.4). 6. Refund method

AP-6.1 Refunds are paid in the booking currency to the original payment method within the period in GLB-8.2. In India, refunds to cards and UPI accounts follow the timelines of the Reserve Bank of India where these are shorter.

AP-6.2 Refunds are never paid in cash at the front desk, except for the unused portion of a cash deposit.

7. Records and request handling

AP-7.1 Incident log entries are kept in the property's working language with an English summary of the category, cause, start and end times and the action taken, so that requests can be assessed consistently across the region.

AP-7.2 Requests received after check-out are acknowledged within one business day. Where the decision depends on a hotel rule written in Japanese, the English summary of the clause relied upon is included for the guest's information; the Japanese text remains authoritative.
