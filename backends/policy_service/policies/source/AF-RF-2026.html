Africa Regional Refund Addendum

Regional rules for our African hotels and lodges, including power supply, water and safari activities.

1. Purpose and scope

AF-1.1 This addendum applies to all ReverieEscapes properties in South Africa (ZA), Kenya (KE), Morocco (MA) and Egypt (EG): Cape Town Waterfront, Johannesburg Sandton, Nairobi Karen, Maasai Mara Safari Lodge, Marrakech Medina and Cairo Nile Corniche.

AF-1.2 It supplements the Global Guest Refund and Compensation Policy (GLB-RF-2026). Country addenda and hotel rules prevail over it for the matters they address (GLB-2.1); where it is silent, the global policy applies.

AF-1.3 Africa Regional Operations may issue country seasonal protocols in response to sustained utility emergencies. Such protocols prevail over this addendum within their effective windows for the matters they address (GLB-2.2).

2. Power supply and load-shedding

AF-2.1 Grid power interruptions, including scheduled load-shedding, are an exception to GLB-5.7. Where the property's backup generation keeps lighting, lifts and water running but in-room climate control is unavailable for more than 4 hours between 22:00 and 07:00, the guest receives 20% of the nightly room rate per affected night, instead of the compensation in GLB-5.1.

AF-2.2 If the hotel's generator fails or is not run during a grid interruption, GLB-5.1 applies in full.

AF-2.3 The maintenance log records the start and end time of each grid interruption, the published utility schedule where one exists (for example the Eskom load-shedding stage or the Kenya Power notice) and the loads carried by backup generation. Entries in this form are verified incidents for the purpose of GLB-4.3.

AF-2.4 Properties inform arriving guests of any announced load-shedding schedule and of the services maintained during interruptions. This information does not reduce the compensation due under AF-2.1 or AF-2.2.

3. Water supply

AF-3.1 Municipal water restrictions are not compensated unless hot water is unavailable for more than 8 hours, in which case GLB-5.2 applies.

AF-3.2 Water-saving measures that the property applies during declared restrictions (reduced linen changes, pool closures, flow limiters) are not service failures, provided guests were informed at check-in.

4. Safari and excursion activities

AF-4.1 Where a safari, game drive or excursion booked through the property is cancelled by the lodge for operational reasons (vehicle, guide or staffing), 100% of the activity fee is refunded. Where it is cancelled for weather or wildlife safety, the guest receives a credit of 50% of the activity fee for use during the same stay or a future stay within 12 months.

AF-4.2 Park, reserve and conservancy fees paid to third parties are refunded only where the property recovers them from the authority concerned. Activities booked directly with external operators are outside this addendum.

5. Payment and currency

AF-5.1 Refunds are paid in the booking currency (GLB-8.1). For bookings in ZAR, KES or EGP paid with a card issued outside the country of the property, the refund may be made in USD at the bank's rate on the day of processing where the card scheme or exchange-control rules require it.

AF-5.2 Refunds are issued within the period in GLB-8.2. Where exchange-control approval is needed, Guest Relations informs the guest of the expected date in writing.
