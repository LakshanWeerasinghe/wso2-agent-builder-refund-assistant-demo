Global Guest Refund and Compensation Policy

Group-wide rules for compensating guests whose stay was affected by a service failure.

1. Purpose and scope

GLB-1.1 This policy sets out when and how ReverieEscapes Hotels & Resorts compensates a guest whose stay was affected by a failure of the room, its equipment or the hotel's services. It applies to every property operated or managed under the ReverieEscapes brand, so that guests in comparable situations are treated alike in every hotel.

GLB-1.2 The policy covers compensation for stays that have been completed or are in progress. Cancellations, rebooking, deposits and the terms of non-refundable rates are governed by the booking conditions and are outside its scope, except where a seasonal policy expressly provides otherwise.

2. Policy hierarchy and precedence

GLB-2.1 Refund rules are layered. Where documents address the same matter, the most specific prevails, in this order: hotel rules, then the country addendum, then the regional addendum, then this global policy.

GLB-2.2 Seasonal policies apply only to nights inside their effective window, and prevail over base documents of the same or broader scope for the matters they address. A more specific base document prevails over a broader seasonal document unless the seasonal document expressly says otherwise.

GLB-2.3 Where a more specific document is silent on a matter, the broader document applies.

GLB-2.4 The versions in force on each night of the stay apply. A seasonal document may expressly apply to decisions made after its issue date for earlier nights inside its window.

GLB-2.5 The original-language version of a document is authoritative; translations are provided for information only.

3. Definitions

GLB-3.1 Nightly room rate means the room price charged for a given night, excluding taxes, service charges, resort or destination fees, and food, beverage or activity charges. Night means a night of the stay, identified by its calendar date. An overnight incident is attributed to the night on which it began; a daytime incident to the night beginning on the same date.

GLB-3.2 Incident means an interruption or failure recorded in the property's maintenance or incident log. Verified means recorded in that log or confirmed in writing by the duty manager. Hotel-caused means arising from the property's own equipment, works, maintenance or events; external means any other cause.

GLB-3.3 Equivalent room means a room of the same or a higher category, bed configuration and occupancy.

4. Eligibility

GLB-4.1 Requests must be made within 30 days of check-out. Requests received later are declined, without prejudice to statutory rights.

GLB-4.2 Cancelled reservations and no-shows are excluded from this policy.

GLB-4.3 Only verified incidents (recorded in the property's maintenance or incident log, or confirmed by the duty manager) are compensated. Claims that cannot be matched to a verified incident receive no refund; the guest is told which records were checked.

GLB-4.4 Where an incident was not reported during the stay although reporting was reasonably possible, compensation is limited to at most 50% of the entitlement.

GLB-4.5 An incident recorded by the property in its own log at the time it occurred is treated as reported for the purpose of GLB-4.4.

5. Compensation schedule

GLB-5.1 Climate control (air conditioning or heating) unavailable for more than 4 hours between 22:00 and 07:00: 50% of the nightly room rate per affected night.

GLB-5.2 Hot water unavailable for more than 4 hours: 30% of the nightly room rate per affected night.

GLB-5.3 Room not cleaned to standard and not put right within 2 hours of the guest's report: 100% of the first night's room rate.

GLB-5.4 Hotel-caused noise (works, maintenance or hotel events) between 22:00 and 07:00: 25% of the nightly room rate per affected night. Daytime noise is not compensated unless a local rule provides otherwise.

GLB-5.5 Facility closures: fees charged for the facility are refunded for the affected days. Room-rate compensation is due only where a local rule designates the facility as a primary facility of the property.

GLB-5.6 Where the guest was moved on the same day to an equivalent or better room, no compensation is due for the nights after the move.

GLB-5.7 External causes (municipal utility outages, external construction, weather, other guests) are not compensated unless a regional, country, hotel or seasonal rule provides otherwise.

GLB-5.8 Where several incidents affect the same night, only the highest single percentage applies; percentages are not added together.

GLB-5.9 Percentages apply to the nightly room rate, excluding taxes and fees.

6. Limits

GLB-6.1 The total refund for a stay never exceeds the total room charges of that stay.

GLB-6.2 Taxes and fees are refunded only where a regional, country or hotel rule requires it.

GLB-6.3 Consequential losses (travel costs, missed events, meals taken elsewhere) are not compensated.

7. Approval authority

GLB-7.1 A Guest Relations representative may approve refunds up to USD 500 equivalent per stay.

GLB-7.2 Refunds above USD 500 and up to USD 2,000 equivalent require the approval of the duty manager.

GLB-7.3 Refunds above USD 2,000 equivalent require the approval of the regional director.

GLB-7.4 Equivalents are calculated at the policy reference rates in Appendix A. More specific documents may set local thresholds in the booking currency, which then apply instead.

GLB-7.5 The approval level is determined by the total refund for the stay, including any refunded fees and taxes. Refunds may not be split to stay within a lower authority.

8. Refund method and timing

GLB-8.1 Refunds are paid in the booking currency to the original payment method.

GLB-8.2 Refunds are issued within 10 business days of approval, unless a regional rule sets a shorter period.

9. Guest communication

GLB-9.1 Decisions are communicated in the guest's preferred language, with the outcome, amount and reasons.

GLB-9.2 The guest receives copies of the policy documents relied upon in the decision.

GLB-9.3 A decision under this policy does not limit the guest's statutory rights, including the right to complain to a consumer authority.
