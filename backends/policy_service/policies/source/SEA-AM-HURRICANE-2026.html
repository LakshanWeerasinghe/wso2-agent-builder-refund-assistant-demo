Atlantic Hurricane Season Guest Policy 2026

Seasonal rules for resort fees, room-rate compensation, early departures and evacuations at coastal Americas properties during the 2026 Atlantic hurricane season.

Seasonal policy — applies only to nights from 1 June 2026 to 30 November 2026.

1. Purpose and scope

HUR-1.1 This policy applies during the 2026 Atlantic hurricane season (1 June to 30 November 2026) to the coastal properties of the Americas region exposed to tropical cyclones: ReverieEscapes Miami Beach (Miami-Dade County, Florida, United States) and ReverieEscapes Cancún Riviera (Benito Juárez, Quintana Roo, Mexico). It sets how guests are compensated when tropical storms or hurricanes affect their stay. Other Americas properties are not covered.

HUR-1.2 This policy supplements the Global Guest Refund and Compensation Policy (GLB-RF-2026) and the Americas Regional Refund Addendum (AM-RF-2026). In accordance with GLB-2.2 it prevails over those documents for the matters it addresses, and it expressly provides an exception to GLB-5.7 for the weather-related causes described in section 2. Matters it does not address remain governed by the base documents.

HUR-1.3 Definitions. A Watch is a Tropical Storm Watch or Hurricane Watch, and a Warning is a Tropical Storm Warning or Hurricane Warning, issued by the U.S. National Hurricane Center (NHC) for the coastal zone that includes the property's county or municipality (for CUN-RIV, the NHC advisory for the Quintana Roo coast, as also published by the Servicio Meteorológico Nacional). A night is inside a Warning when the Warning is in force at any time between 22:00 on that date and 07:00 the following morning, hotel-local time. A day is a calendar day of the stay from the arrival date up to, but excluding, the departure date.

HUR-1.4 The start and end times of each Watch and Warning are taken from the NHC public advisory and recorded by the duty manager in the incident log. Closures of facilities and storm-related utility outages are verified from the property's incident and maintenance logs (GLB-4.3).

2. Guest compensation

HUR-2.1 The resort or destination fee is waived and refunded for each day on which facilities included in the fee (pool, beach service, cabanas, fitness centre or other amenities named in the fee) are closed because of a Tropical Storm or Hurricane Watch or Warning, regardless of how long the closure lasted on that day. This applies in place of the 50% threshold of AM-2.1.

HUR-2.2 For each night inside an active NHC Hurricane Warning or Tropical Storm Warning for the property's county or municipality, the guest receives 20% of the nightly room rate. A Watch alone does not give rise to room-rate compensation.

HUR-2.3 A guest who departs early while a Warning is in force receives a full refund of the unused nights, including nights booked at non-refundable rates. No early-departure fee is charged.

HUR-2.4 Storm-related power outages (utility failures caused by the storm, including where the generator carries life-safety loads only) that leave in-room climate control unavailable for more than 4 hours overnight (between 22:00 and 07:00) are compensated under GLB-5.1, as an exception to GLB-5.7. GLB-5.8 still applies: where a night qualifies under both HUR-2.2 and this clause, only the highest single percentage is paid.

HUR-2.5 Where civil authorities order a mandatory evacuation of the property's zone, the guest receives a full refund of all unused nights plus 100% of the first affected night (the night on which the evacuation order took effect, or the night immediately before it where the order took effect during the day).

HUR-2.6 Fee waivers under HUR-2.1 are paid in addition to any room-rate compensation for the same day; GLB-5.8 applies only to the room-rate percentages. Percentages apply to the nightly room rate excluding taxes and fees (GLB-5.9), and the limits of GLB-6.1 continue to apply.

HUR-2.7 Exclusions: nights before the first Watch or Warning and nights after its cancellation are not compensated under this policy; incidents unrelated to the storm are assessed under the base documents; and reservations cancelled before arrival are handled under the reservation terms, not under this policy (GLB-4.2).

3. Approval, loyalty and processing

HUR-3.1 Refunds under this policy are approved according to GLB-7 (representative up to USD 500 equivalent, duty manager up to USD 2,000, regional director above); MXN amounts are converted at the reference rates in Appendix A of GLB-RF-2026 (AM-5.1).

HUR-3.2 Reverie Circle service-recovery points are awarded under GLB-LC-2026 for each night compensated under HUR-2.2, HUR-2.4 or HUR-2.5. Fee waivers under HUR-2.1 and refunds of unused nights under HUR-2.3 do not by themselves count as affected nights. This policy awards no additional points.

HUR-3.3 Refunds are made to the original payment method within 5–10 business days of approval (AM-3.1). Occupancy and sales taxes on refunded amounts are adjusted by the payment processor and are not part of the compensation amount (AM-3.2). Guests in Mexico keep their rights before PROFECO (AM-4.1).

4. Guest communication and operations

HUR-4.1 When a Watch is issued, the property informs in-house guests and arrivals for the next 72 hours in writing of the expected closures, the guest's options under this policy and the location of shelter areas inside the hotel.

HUR-4.2 When a Warning is issued, the front office offers every in-house guest the early-departure option of HUR-2.3 and records the guest's choice. Guests who stay are given the closure schedule and updated at least twice a day.

HUR-4.3 Decisions under this policy are communicated in the guest's preferred language (GLB-9.1) and name the advisory relied upon. The guest receives a copy of this policy with the other documents relied upon (GLB-9.2).
