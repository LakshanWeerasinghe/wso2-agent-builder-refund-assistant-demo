South Africa Grid Emergency Compensation Protocol

Emergency seasonal protocol raising guest compensation for grid power interruptions at South African properties during sustained Stage 4 and higher load-shedding.

Seasonal policy — applies only to nights from 1 October 2026 to 31 December 2026, including decisions made on or after 14 November 2026 for earlier nights in this window.

1. Purpose and scope

ZAG-1.1 This protocol applies to all ReverieEscapes properties in South Africa (ReverieEscapes Cape Town Waterfront and ReverieEscapes Johannesburg Sandton) for nights from 1 October 2026 to 31 December 2026. It applies to every refund decision made on or after its issue date of 14 November 2026 for nights inside that window, including stays completed before the issue date and requests already received but not yet decided. This retroactive application is express, as permitted by GLB-2.4.

ZAG-1.2 Africa Regional Operations issues this protocol because sustained load-shedding at Stage 4 and above since late September 2026 has left guests without in-room air conditioning for extended overnight periods during the early summer, with an impact on guest comfort that the regional rate of AF-2.1 no longer reflects.

ZAG-1.3 This protocol supplements the Global Guest Refund and Compensation Policy (GLB-RF-2026), the Reverie Circle Loyalty Service Recovery Policy (GLB-LC-2026) and the Africa Regional Refund Addendum (AF-RF-2026). In accordance with GLB-2.2 it prevails over those documents for the matters it addresses. All other matters remain governed by the base documents.

ZAG-1.4 Definitions. A grid interruption is any loss of supply from the national or municipal grid, whether scheduled load-shedding at any stage announced by the national utility or an unscheduled outage. Overnight means between 22:00 and 07:00, hotel-local time. Backup generation is the property's standby generator and battery systems.

2. Compensation for grid interruptions

ZAG-2.1 Where a grid interruption leaves in-room climate control (air conditioning or heating) unavailable for more than 4 hours overnight, while backup generation keeps lighting, lifts and water running, the guest receives 40% of the nightly room rate for each affected night. In South Africa this replaces the 20% rate of AF-2.1 for nights inside the window.

ZAG-2.2 Where the property's generator fails or is not run during a grid interruption, GLB-5.1 applies in full (50% of the nightly room rate per affected night), as already provided by AF-2.2. This protocol does not change that rule.

ZAG-2.3 Scheduled load-shedding is recorded by the property in its maintenance log from the published schedule and the generator run log. Such interruptions are verified incidents under GLB-4.3, and the guest is not required to report each night separately for the purposes of GLB-4.4.

ZAG-2.4 GLB-5.8 continues to apply: where several incidents occur on the same night, only the highest single percentage is paid. Percentages apply to the nightly room rate excluding taxes and fees (GLB-5.9). Where the guest is moved the same day to an equivalent room with working climate control, GLB-5.6 applies.

ZAG-2.5 Interruptions of 4 hours or less overnight, daytime interruptions and municipal water restrictions are not compensated under this protocol; they remain subject to GLB-5.7 and AF-3.1.

3. Reverie Circle recognition

ZAG-3.1 Reverie Circle Gold and Platinum members receive double the service-recovery points of GLB-LC-2026 for each night compensated under ZAG-2.1. Doubled points remain subject to the cap of 15,000 points per stay (LC-3.1).

ZAG-3.2 Classic and Silver members receive points under GLB-LC-2026 without change. Points are posted within 72 hours of approval (LC-4.2) and have no cash value (LC-4.1).

4. Approval authority and processing

ZAG-4.1 Guest relations representatives may approve grid-related refunds up to USD 650 equivalent per stay (ZAR 11,765 at the reference rate in Appendix A of GLB-RF-2026). Above that amount, the duty manager approves up to USD 2,000 equivalent and the regional director above, as in GLB-7.

ZAG-4.2 Refunds are made in the booking currency to the original payment method within 10 business days of approval (GLB-8). Foreign-issued cards on ZAR bookings may be refunded in USD at the bank's rate (AF-5.1).

ZAG-4.3 Requests remain subject to the 30-day limit of GLB-4.1. Requests for nights inside the window that were decided before 14 November 2026 under AF-2.1 may be reassessed at the guest's request, or proactively by the property, and the difference paid.

5. Communication and review

ZAG-5.1 Properties inform arriving guests at check-in of the current load-shedding stage and schedule and of the compensation available under this protocol. Decisions name this protocol and the nights affected, and the guest receives a copy of it with the other documents relied upon (GLB-9.2).

ZAG-5.2 Africa Regional Operations reviews this protocol monthly against the national utility's outlook. It lapses at the end of the night of 31 December 2026 unless extended by a new version.
