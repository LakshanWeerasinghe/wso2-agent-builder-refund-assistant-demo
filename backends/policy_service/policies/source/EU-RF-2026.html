Europe Regional Refund Addendum

Regional rules supplementing the Global Guest Refund and Compensation Policy for our European hotels.

1. Purpose and scope

EU-1.1 This addendum applies to all ReverieEscapes properties in France (FR), Germany (DE), Spain (ES), Italy (IT), the United Kingdom (GB), the Netherlands (NL) and Portugal (PT): Paris Opéra, Nice Promenade, Berlin Mitte, Munich Altstadt, Barcelona Gòtic, Madrid Retiro, Rome Trastevere, Venice Canal Grande, London Mayfair, Edinburgh Old Town, Amsterdam Canal Ring and Lisbon Alfama.

EU-1.2 It supplements the Global Guest Refund and Compensation Policy (GLB-RF-2026). Country addenda and hotel rules prevail over this addendum for the matters they address (GLB-2.1); where this addendum is silent, the global policy applies (GLB-2.3).

EU-1.3 Seasonal policies issued for the region, such as the European summer peak protocol, apply only within their effective windows and prevail over this addendum for the matters they address (GLB-2.2).

2. Refund processing

EU-2.1 Refunds are processed within 14 calendar days of approval. This period replaces the period in GLB-8.2 for European properties.

EU-2.2 Refunds are issued as a credit note against the original invoice, in euros or, for United Kingdom properties, in pounds sterling. Where the room rate includes VAT, the VAT is contained within the refunded amount and is adjusted on the credit note; no separate VAT amount is added to the compensation.

EU-2.3 Where a refund cannot be returned to the original card or account, Guest Relations may make a SEPA transfer (or a Faster Payments transfer in the United Kingdom) to an account held in the guest's name, after verifying the guest's identity.

3. Approval thresholds and local taxes

EU-3.1 For European properties the following local thresholds apply instead of GLB-7.1 to GLB-7.3: a Guest Relations representative may approve refunds up to EUR 450; the duty manager may approve refunds above EUR 450 up to EUR 1,800; refunds above EUR 1,800 require the regional director. For United Kingdom properties the thresholds are up to GBP 390 for the representative and up to GBP 1,550 for the duty manager, with the regional director above GBP 1,550.

EU-3.2 Local tourist taxes (taxe de séjour, Übernachtungsteuer, impuesto sobre estancias turísticas, imposta di soggiorno, toeristenbelasting, taxa turística and similar) are refunded only where a country addendum or hotel rule says so.

EU-3.3 Thresholds apply to the total refund for the stay, including any refunded taxes and fees (GLB-7.5). A seasonal document may set a higher limit for representatives for the matters it addresses.

4. Package travel and intermediaries

EU-4.1 Where the stay was sold as part of a package travel contract, the guest's rights under Directive (EU) 2015/2302 (in the United Kingdom, the Package Travel and Linked Travel Arrangements Regulations 2018) are exercised against the organiser. The hotel provides the organiser with its incident records and any compensation it has agreed to bear.

EU-4.2 For bookings paid to an online travel agency or travel agent, any refund is paid through that intermediary unless the guest paid the hotel directly. Compensation is calculated on the nightly room rate shown on the hotel's invoice.

5. Guest information and local standards

EU-5.1 Guests may request copies of the policy documents relied upon, in English or in the local language of the property, and receive them within 48 hours of the request.

EU-5.2 Hotel-caused noise is assessed in the night window set by GLB-5.4 (22:00 to 07:00) unless a country addendum or hotel rule sets a different window or provides for daytime noise.

EU-5.3 Every decision informs the guest that it does not affect their statutory rights and that they may contact the competent consumer authority or an approved alternative dispute resolution body in the country of the property.

EU-5.4 Incident records and refund files are personal data. They are processed under the General Data Protection Regulation (in the United Kingdom, the UK GDPR) for the purpose of handling the request and retained for six years for accounting purposes.
