Americas Regional Refund Addendum

Regional rules for our hotels and resorts in the United States, Canada and Mexico.

1. Purpose and scope

AM-1.1 This addendum applies to all ReverieEscapes properties in the United States (US), Canada (CA) and Mexico (MX): New York Midtown, Miami Beach, San Francisco Embarcadero, Toronto Harbourfront and Cancún Riviera.

AM-1.2 It supplements the Global Guest Refund and Compensation Policy (GLB-RF-2026). Country addenda and hotel rules prevail over it for the matters they address (GLB-2.1); where it is silent, the global policy applies.

AM-1.3 The Atlantic hurricane season guest policy, issued each year for coastal properties, applies to nights inside its effective window and prevails over this addendum for the matters it addresses (GLB-2.2).

2. Resort fees, destination fees and facilities

AM-2.1 Resort and destination fees are refunded in full for each day on which an amenity included in the fee (pool, beach service, fitness centre or Wi-Fi) was unavailable for more than 50% of its operating hours.

AM-2.2 Room-rate compensation for facility closures follows GLB-5.5: it is due only where a local rule designates the facility as a primary facility of the property.

AM-2.3 The amenities included in each fee and their operating hours are those published in the property's fee disclosure at the time of booking. A day means a calendar day of the stay on which the fee was charged.

AM-2.4 A partial outage that leaves the amenity usable (for example reduced Wi-Fi bandwidth or a closed section of the pool deck) is not an unavailable amenity for the purpose of AM-2.1.

3. Refund method and taxes

AM-3.1 Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days of approval, depending on the card issuer.

AM-3.2 Occupancy and sales taxes on refunded amounts are adjusted by the payment processor and are not part of the compensation amount. The compensation is stated before tax; the guest's statement may therefore show a slightly higher credit.

AM-3.3 Refunds are made in the booking currency (USD, CAD or MXN). Where the guest has an open card dispute for the same charges, Guest Relations coordinates the refund with the dispute so that no amount is refunded twice.

4. Consumer rights

AM-4.1 Guests at properties in Mexico keep their rights before the Procuraduría Federal del Consumidor (PROFECO). Every decision for a Mexican property says so, in Spanish and in the guest's preferred language.

AM-4.2 Guests in the United States and Canada keep their rights under applicable state, provincial and federal consumer protection law. A refund decision is not a waiver of those rights and guests are never asked to sign a release in exchange for compensation.

5. Approval authority

AM-5.1 Approval thresholds follow GLB-7. For CAD and MXN bookings, equivalents are calculated at the policy reference rates in Appendix A of GLB-RF-2026. For information, at the Q4 2026 rates the thresholds correspond to the amounts below; the current Appendix A rates prevail.

AM-5.2 Refunded resort and destination fees count toward the approval thresholds together with any room-rate compensation (GLB-7.5).

6. Request handling and records

AM-6.1 Requests raised at the front desk during the stay may be resolved by the duty manager before check-out within the thresholds in section 5. The resolution is recorded in the refund case so that the same night or fee is not compensated twice.

AM-6.2 Incident log entries for amenity closures record the amenity, the start and end of the closure, the published operating hours and the reason, so that AM-2.1 can be applied day by day. Weather-related closures also record the official warning in force, if any.

AM-6.3 Requests received by email or telephone after check-out are acknowledged within one business day, and the guest is told the expected date of the decision.
