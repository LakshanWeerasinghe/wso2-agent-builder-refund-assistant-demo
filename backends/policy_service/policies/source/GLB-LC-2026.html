Reverie Circle Loyalty Service Recovery Policy

Bonus points for Reverie Circle members whose stay fell short of our standards.

1. Purpose, scope and definitions

LC-1.1 This policy sets out the Reverie Circle bonus points awarded, in addition to any monetary compensation, when a member's stay is affected by a service failure. It complements the Global Guest Refund and Compensation Policy (GLB-RF-2026) and applies at every ReverieEscapes property.

LC-1.2 The policy applies to stays booked under a valid Reverie Circle membership number. Guests who are not members at the time of the stay do not receive service recovery points; Guest Relations may invite them to enrol, but no points are awarded retroactively.

LC-1.3 Affected night means a night of the stay for which room-rate compensation is approved under the applicable refund policies. A refund limited to fees, taxes, service charges or activity charges does not create an affected night. Tier means the member's Reverie Circle tier on the date the compensation is approved.

2. Service recovery points

LC-2.1 Service recovery points are awarded only when compensation is approved, and are calculated per affected night. No points are awarded where a request is declined, including where the incident could not be verified or the request was made outside the eligibility period.

LC-2.2 Classic members: 0 points per affected night.

LC-2.3 Silver members: 1,000 points per affected night.

LC-2.4 Gold members: 2,500 points per affected night.

LC-2.5 Platinum members: 5,000 points per affected night.

LC-2.6 Points are awarded once per affected night, regardless of the number of incidents recorded on that night or the percentage of compensation approved for it.

3. Caps and certificates

LC-3.1 Service recovery points are capped at 15,000 points per stay, including any extra points awarded under seasonal or local rules.

LC-3.2 A Platinum member with 3 or more affected nights in one stay also receives one Complimentary Night certificate.

LC-3.3 A Complimentary Night certificate is valid for one night in a standard room for two guests at any ReverieEscapes property, subject to availability, for 12 months from issue. It excludes taxes, fees and incidental charges and cannot be extended or exchanged.

LC-3.4 A stay booked as several consecutive reservations at the same property is treated as one stay for the cap in LC-3.1.

4. Nature and posting of points

LC-4.1 Service recovery points have no cash value, cannot be transferred or exchanged for money, and are not tier-qualifying. A member may not choose points instead of a monetary refund.

LC-4.2 Points are posted to the member's account within 72 hours of approval of the compensation.

LC-4.3 If an approved compensation is later reversed, for example after a payment dispute or the discovery of incorrect records, the related points and certificates are reversed as well.

LC-4.4 Service recovery points are otherwise subject to the Reverie Circle programme terms, including the rules on expiry.

5. Seasonal and local rules

LC-5.1 Seasonal or local rules may award extra points, for example a multiplier or a fixed amount per night. Extra points count toward the cap in LC-3.1 unless the rule expressly states otherwise.

LC-5.2 Where a seasonal or local rule awards extra points, Guest Relations records the rule relied upon and the calculation in the refund case, so that the award can be audited.

6. Administration

LC-6.1 The colleague who approves the compensation also approves the related points; no separate loyalty approval is required. The points awarded are stated in the decision sent to the member.

LC-6.2 Members who believe points were not posted correctly may contact the Reverie Circle service centre within 90 days of the decision.
